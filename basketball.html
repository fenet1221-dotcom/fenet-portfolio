My Basketball Journey

I have been playing basketball for more than eight years, developing my skills through training, competition, teamwork, and leadership.

Basketball has been an important part of my life and has taught me discipline, resilience, responsibility, and how to work with others.

Playing Experience

Addis Basketball Academy

I have played with Addis Basketball Academy for more than eight years, primarily as a point guard and shooter.

Throughout my playing journey, I have competed in different competitions while continuing to develop my individual skills and understanding of the game.

Leadership

I have served as a team captain for most of my basketball years. Being a captain has taught me how to communicate with teammates, take responsibility, support others, and lead by example.

Leadership, to me, is about helping the team stay focused, encouraging teammates, and taking responsibility when things become difficult.
